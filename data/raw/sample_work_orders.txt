## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document contains three fictional maintenance work orders for the demonstration equipment identified as FIQ-IMM-001.

The records are synthetic examples created to test ForgeIQ's ability to retrieve historical events, summarize recorded observations, distinguish findings from suspected causes, and identify incomplete records.

No work described in this document is claimed to have occurred at a real facility.

These records do not establish approved troubleshooting procedures, equipment operating limits, or safety instructions.

## 5. Historical Record Interpretation

Work orders document individual events and the information recorded about them.

A reported symptom is not necessarily a confirmed fault. An observed condition does not automatically establish a root cause. A proposed corrective action does not establish that the action was completed.

When answering questions about historical maintenance, ForgeIQ should distinguish between:

* What was reported.
* What was directly observed.
* What was assessed.
* What cause, if any, was confirmed.
* What corrective action was documented.
* Whether the action was completed.
* Whether a verification outcome was recorded.
* The work order's recorded status.

If a field is absent or explicitly marked as not recorded, ForgeIQ must not invent a value.

## 6. Cross-Record Analysis

The three sample work orders contain different reported symptoms:

* FIQ-WO-001 records unusual hydraulic noise.
* FIQ-WO-002 records an abnormal temperature indication.
* FIQ-WO-003 records visible hydraulic leakage.

All three records lack a documented verification outcome.

The records do not establish that the three events share a common cause, that any component failed, or that any corrective action resolved the reported condition.

ForgeIQ may summarize these documented patterns, but it must not infer a confirmed recurring failure or common root cause without supporting evidence.
